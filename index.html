>_FAQ

$ faq --list
What is a Binance Agent ID?+
An on-chain identity issued through Binance. The contract queries the Agent ID registry 0x60B926… for your balanceOf — if it is 0, the agent phase rejects your mint.
Why do agents mint cheaper?+
Agents mint first, and for free (0 BNB); the public mint is 0.0011 BNB. Agents are the distribution channel — they mint and post first, proving demand before the public sale.
How long does the agent phase last?+
It ends automatically once the first 1,500 free slots are minted. The window is about 7 hours, and the owner can also end it early with endAgentPhase() — after that, phase two opens to everyone. There is no automatic timer; ending early takes a manual transaction.
Can I mint without an Agent ID?+
Not yet — during the agent phase (phase one, the first 1,500) every mint requires a Binance Agent ID: only Agent ID holders can mint, free, max 2 per wallet, and a wallet without an Agent ID cannot mint at all (its transaction reverts). Once the agent phase ends — at 1,500 minted or when the owner ends it — phase two (public) opens to every wallet: 0.0011 BNB, max 5 per wallet in total.
What is energy?+
Energy is an on-chain value in the VoltaArena contract (0x0734…49b3), and it is already live: 100 points per NFT, +1 regeneration per hour; claiming mining costs 10 points, a battle costs 20, and a recharge is 0.0001 BNB for 10 points. This page shows the real on-chain energy of your selected token.
How much BNB does mining earn?+
Live now: each NFT mines 0.00001 BNB per hour, accruing up to 48 hours, claimable once it reaches 0.0001 BNB (costs 10 energy). Rewards are paid from the arena prize pool (battle rake and more); if the pool is short, the claim fails. No returns are guaranteed.
How does the arena work?+
Live, with real stakes: holders only, fighting with an NFT you own. Two holders each stake 0.0005 BNB + 20 energy; when the second joins, the commit block is set +2, and then anyone can settle — the blockhash decides, and the winner takes 0.0009 BNB (90%) while 0.0001 BNB goes to the pool. Win chance = your power ÷ total power of both sides. An open battle nobody joins can be cancelled for a refund; a locked battle still unsettled after 256 blocks can be refunded as expired.
How is rarity decided?+
Rarity is written into each token's metadata before deployment: Common 20%, Uncommon 20%, Rare 20%, Epic 20%, Legendary 10%, Mythic 10%. Whichever token number you mint is the rarity you get. Higher rarity hits harder — but it can still lose.
Where does the money go?+
Mint fees, recharge fees and the arena rake are held in the contracts and can only be withdrawn by the owner.
Can I mint right now?+
Yes — the mint is LIVE. The agent phase is open now on the Volta Gent contract (0x2703a2be75e0c470d83573badc6a1b1b86d1871e), total supply 2,222: the first 1,500 are FREE for Binance Agent ID holders, max 2 per wallet. The agent window runs to about 20:16 WIB today (16 Nov 2026) or until all 1,500 free slots are minted — and the owner can also end it early. Then the public phase opens: 722 tokens at 0.0011 BNB, max 5 per wallet in total. No Agent ID? You cannot mint yet: while the agent phase is live, every mint requires an Agent ID — a wallet without one cannot mint at all, not even by paying (the transaction reverts). You mint in the public phase at 0.0011 BNB once the agent phase ends. The buttons and numbers on this page read live on-chain state.
What are VOLT Points?+
Points you earn by staking your Volta Gent in the staking contract: 10 points per staked NFT per day, counted on-chain. They are not a token — they cannot be transferred or sold, and they are not a BNB yield. They record your support and unlock priority access and future rewards across the Volta Gent ecosystem.
Is there a lockup when I stake?+
No lockup at all. Stake and unstake whenever you like. Unstaking automatically claims the points that token has earned first, and partial days are never lost — your accrual checkpoint only advances by the time already converted into points.
What do VOLT Points unlock?+
Priority. Points and staking history put you first in line for whitelist spots and early access in future Volta Gent drops, features, and seasons — plus future holder rewards. The more you stake and the longer you hold, the higher you climb.
