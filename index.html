>_ FAQ.

$ faq --list
What is a Binance Agent ID?+
An on-chain identity from Binance. The contract checks balanceOf on the Agent ID registry 0x60B926… — if it is 0, the Agent phase rejects your mint.
Why do Agents pay less?+
Agents mint first at 0.0001 BNB; the public pays 0.001 BNB. Agents are the distribution — they mint, post, and prove demand before the public sale.
How long is the Agent phase?+
7 hours. After that the owner calls endAgentPhase() and Phase 2 opens to everyone. No automatic timer — one explicit transaction.
Can I mint without an Agent ID?+
Yes, in Phase 2 (public): 0.001 BNB, max 5 per wallet. Phase 1 is Agent-only.
What is energy?+
Every NFT has 100 energy. It regens +1/hour free. Claiming costs 10, battling costs 20. Recharge is 0.0001 BNB per 10 energy, or 0.001 BNB for a full refill.
How does the arena work?+
Two holders stake 0.0005 BNB + 20 energy each. Winner takes 0.0009 BNB (90%), the project keeps 0.0001 BNB. Win chance = your tier power ÷ total power.
How are tiers decided?+
Drawn at mint and stored on-chain: Common 50%, Uncommon 25%, Rare 15%, Epic 7%, Legendary 2.5%, Mythic 0.5%. Stronger tiers fight better — but they can still lose.
Where does the money go?+
Mint fees, recharge fees, and the arena cut are held in the contract. Only the owner can withdraw.
Is mint open now?+
No. This is a preview — no contract deployed, no charge, mint locked until the owner opens it.
